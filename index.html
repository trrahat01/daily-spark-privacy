Daily Spark - Privacy Policy

# Privacy Policy for Daily Spark

Effective date: August 8, 2026

Daily Spark is a motivational quotes app designed to help users read, browse, and
save inspirational quotes.

## Information We Collect

Daily Spark does not require users to create an account for normal quote browsing.

The app may store favorite quotes locally on your device so you can access them again
later. This information stays on your device and is not sold.

## Backend Services

Daily Spark may connect to a Supabase backend to load quote content and app data.
Network communication is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

## Advertising

Daily Spark is free and supported by third-party advertising provided by Google
AdMob. AdMob may use your device advertising ID to serve and measure ads, including
personalized ads, subject to your device settings and Google's advertising choices.

Ads are served with frequency capping to minimize interruption. You can opt out of
personalized advertising at any time by adjusting the "Ads" setting in your device or
through Google's Ads Settings page.

AdMob's processing of data is governed by Google's privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Permissions

The public user version of Daily Spark does not require location, camera, microphone,
or media library permissions for normal use.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Advertising partners (AdMob) may receive an
advertising identifier to serve ads, as described above.

## Children

Daily Spark is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and remove it
if necessary.

## Data Deletion

Because the public user app does not require account creation, there is no account data
to delete. You can remove locally stored favorites by uninstalling the app or clearing
the app data from your device settings.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on the
public privacy policy page.
